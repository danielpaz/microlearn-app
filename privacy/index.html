Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Who we are

Microlearn (www.microlearn.website) is an independent educational project with free interactive micro-lessons. The Microlearn app (app.microlearn.website), offered through the Google Workspace Marketplace, adds Google sign-in, a certified exam and certificates. Contact: microlearn@googlegroups.com.

What we collect

On the public site we collect no personal data. Your lesson progress, theme and language stay only in your browser (local storage).

In the app, when you sign in with Google, we receive from Google your name, e-mail address, Google account ID and, for Google Workspace accounts, your organization's domain. We do not receive your password.

While you use the app we store which lessons you completed, your certified exam attempts (questions drawn, answers, score and dates) and your language.

Certificates and payments (when available): the certificate code, the name printed on it, the course, score and date; for payments, only the payment reference from Stripe. Card data is handled by Stripe and never reaches Microlearn.

Why we use it

To sign you in, record your progress, run and grade the certified exam, issue and verify certificates, manage school licenses, and answer your requests. We do not sell your data, do not show ads and do not use your data to train AI models.

Google user data

We only request the OpenID scopes openid, email and profile (and, when certificates are issued, drive.file, which only lets the app create and open the certificate files it saves in your Google Drive). Microlearn's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored in Google Workspace services (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets) under the Microlearn account. Stripe processes payments. The app pages are served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which may log technical data such as IP address to deliver and protect the site.

How long we keep it

Progress and exam data are kept while your account is in use. Certificate records are kept so that anyone can verify a certificate you share; you can ask us to revoke and delete them. Payment references are kept as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by writing to microlearn@googlegroups.com. These rights include those under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and similar laws. You can also remove the app's access at any time in your Google account.

Schools and minors

When a school uses Microlearn with Google Workspace for Education accounts, the school's administrator installs the app and is responsible for authorizing its use by students. The certificate verification page shows only the printed name, course, date and status.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the app.
