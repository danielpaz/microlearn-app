Free learning

Lessons, labs, the Nano test and the certified exam are free. You do not need an account to learn on the public site.

Account

The certified exam and certificates require signing in with a Google account in the app. You are responsible for your account and for the information you provide, including the name printed on your certificate (which you can correct once before issuing).

Certified exam

The certified exam opens after you complete all lessons of the course in the app. Each attempt has 20 questions drawn by the server; you pass with 80 out of 100. You have 3 attempts per round; if you do not pass, a new round of 3 attempts opens 30 days after the last one. An attempt counts as soon as it starts. Taking the exam for someone else, sharing answers or using automated tools is not allowed and may lead to revocation of certificates.

Certificates and prices

After passing, you can issue the course certificate for US$ 0.99 per certificate, paid when issuing (several pending certificates can be paid together). Schools can buy a US$ 99.99 license for 1 year, renewable, with unlimited certificates for all accounts of the school's Google Workspace domain. Payments are processed by Stripe. Because you only pay after passing, payments are final, except where the law grants a refund. Certificates already issued remain valid after a school license ends.

What a certificate means

Microlearn certificates are certificates of completion of a free course (in Brazil, curso livre). They have no academic validity and are not degrees, diplomas or professional licenses. The workload shown is an estimate. Each certificate has a code that anyone can check on the verification page.
